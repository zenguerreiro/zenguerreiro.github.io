Selected projects

Built from
first principles.

Four projects from a decade at Enerkem Alberta Biofuels, the world's first full-scale municipal waste-to-biofuels plant, where there was no prior full-scale plant to learn from.

01

Phase 1: Methanol

Enerkem Alberta Biofuels · Greenfield commissioning

Greenfield commissioning of a novel, proprietary waste-to-methanol process, taking 15+ process units from pre-mechanical completion through to first production.

  • Commissioning operator on the original greenfield startup
  • Led a team of instrumentation technicians through instrument commissioning, tracking punch items and prioritizing work for system readiness
  • Lead DCS Operator for commissioning and startup of the gasifier, compressors, cooling tower and all instrumentation
  • Worked with engineers on design changes that resolved repeated startup failures on technology with no commercial-scale precedent

Outcome

First methanol

Achieved in 2015 on a first-of-its-kind plant.

02

Phase 1.5: Expansion

Enerkem Alberta Biofuels · Capacity expansion

An expansion project that doubled the throughput of the methanol plant.

  • Team lead for commissioning of the expansion scope
  • Managed punch lists, line walkdowns, PSSRs and handover of new equipment to operations
  • Generated non-conformance reports and completed punch items including flange retorquing and tubing installation

Outcome

2× throughput

Methanol plant capacity doubled. The plant produced 38M litres of methanol in 2017.

03

Phase 2: Ethanol

Enerkem Alberta Biofuels · Process extension

Extending the plant from methanol to ethanol production by commissioning new downstream units, including carbonylation, hydrogenolysis and ethanol distillation.

  • Commissioning supervisor for the Phase 2 units, a role held from Phase 2 onward
  • Led punch list management, line walkdowns and pre-startup safety reviews
  • Partnered with engineering on design changes to get the new process running reliably
  • Later consulted on design changes for two proposed new facilities in Quebec and the Netherlands

Outcome

First ethanol

Achieved in 2018 from municipal solid waste.

04

Building a site LMS from the ground up

Enerkem Alberta Biofuels · Operational readiness

A complete learning management system and procedure framework for a plant with no existing operating history to draw on.

  • Risk-ranked every operating task on site to set training and procedure priorities
  • Wrote and approved SOPs, startup/shutdown procedures, emergency response plans, LOTO procedures and the permitting system
  • Authored operating manuals and training material, and built the system to deliver and track it
  • Implemented and maintained the framework, and reported training compliance and procedure status to leadership

Outcome

Every task

Risk-ranked, proceduralized and backed by tracked training.
