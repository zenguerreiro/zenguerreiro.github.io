Welcome

First-of-a-kind plants. Zero lost-time incidents.

Thanks for stopping by. This site is a short introduction to my work in commissioning, startup and plant operations.

I'm a Commissioning & Startup Specialist with 15 years of process operations experience across refining, chemical and SAGD facilities, including more than ten years focused on commissioning and startup.

For a decade at Enerkem I helped commission the world's first full-scale municipal waste-to-biofuels plant: novel, unproven technology across 15+ process units, taken from pre-mechanical completion through sustained production. Along the way I built the site's learning management system from the ground up and spent six years leading crews of up to 30 people.

Today I commission well pads and capital projects at Suncor's Firebag and MacKay River operations. My work combines hands-on field commissioning with operational readiness: PSSRs, HAZOPs, procedure authoring, training and completions management.

Away from the plant: Brazilian jiu jitsu black belt and coach, freediver, husband and father.
