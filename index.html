About me

I'm a commissioning and start-up specialist based in Edmonton, Alberta. For about 15 years I've worked in refining, chemical plants, and oil sands production, helping bring new units from final construction to safe, steady operation. I recently earned my Third Class Power Engineering certificate.

I was part of the team that started up a first-of-its-kind waste-to-biofuels plant, and I spent six years leading crews of up to 30 people. I care about safety, good training, and teams that look out for each other.

Outside of work, I co-founded Black Sails Brazilian Jiu Jitsu, where I coach adults and kids. The rest of my time belongs to my family.
